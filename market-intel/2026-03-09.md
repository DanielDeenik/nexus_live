### UK IR35 Landscape
Improving for contractors. From April 2026, new company size thresholds will reclassify some medium firms as small, shifting IR35 determination responsibility back to PSCs. This could open more outside-IR35 opportunities, though the practical effect won't materialize until April 2027 due to the two-year look-back period. New Joint & Several Liability rules for umbrella companies also take effect April 2026, which may push more clients toward direct PSC engagements — a positive signal for Dan's preferred outside-IR35 model.

The AIFMD II deadline creates a regulatory crunch alongside organic growth — a double tailwind for interim fund ops professionals.
