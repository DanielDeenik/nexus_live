# Nexus privacy notice

Nexus (the Nexuslive iPhone app and its server) is a personal finance tool. Its owner built it for his own use and is its only user. It is not offered to anyone else.

## What Nexus reads

- Balances and transactions of the owner's own bank and card accounts, through Enable Banking, after the owner approves access in the bank's own app. This is account information only: Nexus cannot make payments or move money.
- Invoices, receipts and order emails from services the owner connects himself, such as his mailbox, his bookkeeping and his broker.

## Where it is kept

- In the owner's Supabase project in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1). Row level security lets only the owner's account read the data.
- Keys and bank sessions are kept in Supabase Vault and are not shown again once saved.

## Sharing

None. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

## How long

- Bank access lasts as long as the consent the owner gave in the bank's app, at most 180 days. It ends at once when he withdraws it there or disconnects the bank in Nexus.
- The data stays until the owner deletes it.
